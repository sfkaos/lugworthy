# 01 PRD — Lugworthy (lugworthy.com)
## Vision
Lugworthy (lugworthy.com) is the deepest, most complete directory of independent and microbrand watches: every brand, every model, every spec, so a shopper never needs to open a brand site to decide. En Punto videos live inside the directory, attached to the watches they feature.

## Users
- Shopper/enthusiast (primary): filters by size, movement, price; compares.
- Brand owner: claims page, controls images, keeps specs right; wants exposure and leads.
- Win/editor (admin): curates, tags videos, reviews ingestion.
- En Punto prospect exhibitor: discovers the fair from brand pages.

## Scope
MVP: brand index, brand detail (themed), model detail with full specs, browse/filter, compare (2–4), video hub + video pages, embedded videos on brand/model pages, editorial articles, claim + image license, Shopify ingestion, admin, English UI (i18n-ready), affiliate-tagged outbound links, newsletter signup.
Later: Spanish locale, user accounts/saved lists, price-drop alerts, reviews, Shopify sales-channel cart handoff (checkout add-on), sponsor placements, brand analytics dashboard, non-Shopify connectors.

## User stories
- As a shopper I filter 36–39mm, automatic, sapphire, 200m+, under a price, ships to US, and see matching models.
- As a shopper I compare 3 divers side by side showing only differences.
- As a shopper on a model page I watch the En Punto clip at the moment that watch appears.
- As a brand I claim my page with my domain email, upload banner/logo/colors, approve images, fix specs.
- As Win I tag a new YouTube video to 4 models with timestamps and it appears on those pages.
- As Win I review ingested drafts and publish them.

## Non-goals
On-site checkout or payments in MVP; marketplace for used watches; non-micro luxury brands; user reviews in MVP; scraping non-Shopify sites without permission.

## Success metrics (see 13)
Monthly visitors, filter usage, outbound clicks per session, claimed brands, En Punto exhibitor applications from site, newsletter subs.
